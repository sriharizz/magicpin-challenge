**Scope**: Full forensic line-by-line audit of all production modules in `app/`  
**Classification Criteria**:
- **SAFE**: Generic schema/semantic behavior that works for arbitrary inputs.
- **QUESTIONABLE**: May generalize, but depends on an unverified schema assumption.
